Keep the Client

Spell out what the client actually said — or what you say back — before they walk. Then earn the How It Helps card.

How to play

  • A client is talking. Guess letters to spell their line — the words they actually used at the booth.
  • Each wrong letter walks them one step closer to the exit. Run out of steps and they leave.
  • Keep them and you earn the How It Helps card — then answer one question on what you just read.
  • Lose them and you get the model line only. The card stays locked until you win that client back.

Three tiers

  • 1 · New on the floor — short client lines, 6 steps, one free letter.
  • 2 · Taking clients — every client line, 5 steps, a hint costs you a step.
  • 3 · Specialist — you spell your own response, 4 steps, no hints.

Rush hour

  • The same clients on a clock. Let it run out and they are gone, however many steps you had left.
The rule this game runs on: never tell a client they have a condition. You are spelling out their words and your response — never a diagnosis. The clinical background on each card is for your understanding only.
