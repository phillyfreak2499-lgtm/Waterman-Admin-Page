How to Play

  1. Envelope holds one Strengthener, one Maintainer, one Relaxer.
  2. Your hand cannot be in the envelope - mark them in the notebook.
  3. Explore rooms for clue drops. Hallways connect rooms like Clue.
  4. Suggest only from a room. Cards you hold or have seen are hidden.
  5. Professor tips rotate each of your rounds.
  6. Wall notes rule whole fit families out - they cross cards off your notebook for you.
  7. Your rivals deduce too - they remember every card they are shown and will accuse the moment they can.
  8. Closing time ends the case after round 10 whether or not anyone has solved it.
  9. Accuse only when sure - a wrong final accusation ends the case. The accusation list always shows every card.
