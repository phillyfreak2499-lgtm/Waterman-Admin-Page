    How to play (and learn)

    • Move: WASD / arrows · phone joystick
    • Act (E / Space / ACT): near booth, door, or backroom
    • Answers: press 1–5 (or click) — big numbered choices
    • Read time: when you open a booth (E / Space), that booth timer pauses
    • Hand-off: bathroom / lunch / phone — floor is never unowned (or press H)
    • Double greet: Connect at the door more than once when you can
    • C Connect: greet at the entrance when a Client arrives
    • A Assess: watch radar + mood bars before you step in
    • R Respond: AAH on reds / amplify WOWs — never hijack
    • E Elevate: backroom coach when a Specialist peels off
    • Grades: Strong / Medium / Weak only after you choose
    • Stamina: sprinting everywhere drains you — path with purpose
    • Floor heat: too many open reds and the whole store tilts

    What you will drill

    • Red-flag plays from CARE training (shoes early, price, spouse, rushed walk, talking through demo)
    • Protect green · amplify WOW · hand back ownership
    • Client types: spouse, price-sensitive, return issue, skeptic, caregiver
    • Mindset: answer fully — do not step down or escape
